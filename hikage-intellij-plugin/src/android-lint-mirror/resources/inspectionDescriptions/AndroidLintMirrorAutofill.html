Reports Hikage text fields that do not provide Autofill metadata.

Specify an autofillHints attribute when targeting SDK version 26 or higher, or explicitly specify that the view is not important for Autofill. This helps an Autofill service classify values such as usernames, passwords, credit card fields, and email addresses.

You can mark a view unimportant for Autofill by specifying importantForAutofill on that view or a parent view.
